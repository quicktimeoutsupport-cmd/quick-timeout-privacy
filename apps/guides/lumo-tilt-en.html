Tilt your phone. Guide the light.

LumoTilt is a single-player marble maze: gently tilt your device to guide a glowing ball to the goal ring. Lumo evokes light and sparkle; Tilt describes the way you play. Start with a small movement of your wrists.

Download LumoTilt · App Store

iPhone · iPad · Free with ads. Check your local age rating. Android is in closed testing.

Your first round

  1. Hold your device upright in portrait at a comfortable viewing angle.
  2. Select tilt controls and start a round. If a starting prompt appears, keep that comfortable angle while it completes.
  3. Tilt gently to move the marble. Small adjustments give you more control than large movements.
  4. Avoid walls and hazards and reach the goal ring. Try collecting gems along the way, and have another go after a miss.

Taking turns with friends

Pass one device to the next person after a round and compare the routes you try. This is single-player play shared by taking turns, not simultaneous multiplayer or an online match.

If controls feel wrong on a landscape iPad

Turn the device upright and restart the round. Some iPad windowing configurations can show a portrait app window inside a landscape display. Align the device and game orientation before playing. As of October 24, 2026, an update improving the starting orientation prompt is waiting for review; it is not yet available to every public user.

Can I use touch controls?

Drag controls are available as an alternative. Check which control mode you selected, since tilt and drag respond differently.
