Save the line. Find it again.

You remember a great line, but not the page. Readmori helps you photograph book passages, select the lines you want, and organize your own notes by book. Core note-taking works on your device without an account.

Preparing for public release. As of October 24, 2026, iOS is waiting for review and Android is in closed testing. Public download links will be added after availability is verified.

From a photo to a reading note

  1. Add a book, then photograph a page or select a photo.
  2. Review the recognized text and correct any mistakes.
  3. Select the lines you want to keep and save your thoughts with them. Multiple selections can be kept in the order you choose them.
  4. Check any detected page number against the book. You can enter it yourself when recognition is uncertain.

Which languages can it recognize?

Text recognition supports Korean and English. The app's interface languages are separate from its OCR capabilities. Lighting, fonts, shadows and curved pages can affect results, so compare the text with the original before saving.

Finding a passage later

Browse notes by book or use search to return to a passage. The focus is keeping the lines that matter to you, rather than retyping entire pages. Readmori does not generate AI book summaries.

Protecting your notes

Notes are stored locally. Create a backup before removing the app or changing devices, and check that you have kept the backup file. Do not assume automatic cloud sync. The app includes ads; its privacy policy explains advertising data processing.
