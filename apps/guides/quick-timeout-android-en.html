Quick Timeout / Android

Change your Android screen timeout for the task at hand

If your screen turns off while you read a recipe or assembly instructions, Quick Timeout lets you choose a longer Android screen timeout.

Download · Google Play

Free · Contains ads

Set up the required access

Android 1.0.2 requires both Modify system settings and Alarms & reminders access before you can start. They are used to change the screen timeout and schedule restoration, respectively. Follow the permission guidance and enable both before returning.

Choose a duration and check auto restore

  1. Choose 10, 30 or 45 minutes.
  2. Check the Auto restore status, then return to what you were reading.
  3. When restoration is scheduled, the timeout returns to the 15-second default after the selected duration. It does not restore a previous custom value.

You can also select the 15-second default yourself. Turning off Auto restore cancels the scheduled restoration; check your screen timeout when finished. The app does not turn a manually locked screen back on or force it to remain awake in every situation.

Using an iPhone or iPad?

iOS uses a PiP status window and does not change the system Auto-Lock preference. Read the iPhone and iPad guide.

Privacy policy

Based on the public Android 1.0.2 release. Check your device settings and permission status.
