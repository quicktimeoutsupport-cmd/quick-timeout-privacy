먹빼 / EAT.RUN. / iPhone · iPad

See today's meals as an estimated running distance

Before your run, enter what you ate today and check the estimated running distance. EAT.RUN. converts calories above your estimated daily energy needs into a distance, using your profile.

Download on the App Store

Free with ads

Get started

Set up your profile, then add your foods and portions. Log each meal as you eat to review your daily intake, or enter your meals together before exercise. Long-press a food shortcut to adjust its portion. You can also search the food library or add your own food.

The distance is 0 km when intake is at or below the estimate. Calories and distance are estimates, not an exercise prescription or a recommendation for how much to eat.

No account is required. Your profile and food records stay on your device, and food logging works offline.
