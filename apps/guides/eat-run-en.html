먹빼 / EAT.RUN. / iPhone · iPad

Log a meal and adjust the portion Tap a food shortcut in EAT.RUN. to log a meal. Set up your profile first, then choose what you ate. Long-press a food shortcut on the home screen to choose a portion. You can also search the food library or add your own food. Your recent food entries help shape the suggestions for the next entry. Use Undo if you add something by mistake. The displayed running distance is an estimate based on your inputs, not an exercise requirement. See the app's data sources for its calculation references. Free with ads. Find the iOS download under EAT.RUN. on the Bonney Apps page. Video captions: “Ate half the chicken. / Logged the whole thing?” → actual portion selection → “Share the chicken.
