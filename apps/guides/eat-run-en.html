EAT.RUN. / iPhone · iPad

Find a food, choose a portion, log your meal

EAT.RUN. is a meal diary for iPhone and iPad. Version 1.0.2 includes 744 foods and drinks, searchable names and aliases, and a visible portion control.

Download · App Store

Free · Contains ads

Log one meal

  1. Start with Portion on the home screen, open Food Library, and search for a food. Search supports registered aliases and differences in spacing.
  2. Select the food and choose an amount. Choosing the portion saves the entry.
  3. Review the entry in today’s meal list.

In regular food search, tapping a food row immediately logs the default portion. To use a different amount, start with Portion on the home screen or use the portion icon beside a food. You can also add a custom food.

Records stay on your device

No account is required. Profiles and meal records are stored on your device, and food search and logging work offline. Advertising services may process device and advertising data separately from meal records.

Estimates, with limits

Calories and running distances are estimates, not an exercise prescription or a weight-loss guarantee. Logged meals do not create an obligation to exercise.
