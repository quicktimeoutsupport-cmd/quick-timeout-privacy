EAT.RUN. / iPhone · iPad

Shoes on. What does today's food look like in kilometres?

EAT.RUN. gives you a playful way to look at today’s food before heading out for a run: an estimated distance in kilometres.

Your estimated daily energy needs come first. Only the calories above that full-day baseline become an approximate running distance. Entering food and portions supplies the numbers for that calculation.

Download EAT.RUN. · App Store

iPhone · iPad · Free · Contains banner ads

See today’s estimated distance

  1. Set up your profile so the app can estimate your daily energy needs.
  2. On Home, choose Portion → Food Library, find a food you ate today, and choose the amount. Selecting the portion saves the entry.
  3. After entering today’s food, check the KM number on Home. Changing a food or its amount updates the estimate.

Version 1.0.2 includes 744 foods and drinks, searchable by local names and registered aliases. Tapping a frequent-food button or a regular search result adds its default portion. Start with Portion to choose another amount, or add a custom food.

A number to take lightly

Intake at or below the full-day baseline displays 0 km. The result is an estimate, not a distance you must run or a training prescription. EAT.RUN. does not track GPS routes or calculate running time.

How the calculation works and what 0 km means

No account required

Your profile and food entries stay on your device. Food search and calculation work offline. The app is free with banner ads; advertising services may process device and ad data separately.

Privacy policy

Based on public iOS 1.0.2. Android remains in closed testing.
