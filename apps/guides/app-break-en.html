App Break / Android

Put a limit on the apps that keep you scrolling

One more video can turn into a later bedtime. App Break helps you pause selected Android apps when you reach the limits you set.

Get it on Google Play

Free with ads

Get started

Start with one app. Follow the permission guidance, try a short limit to check the pause prompt, then choose the limit you want to use. If the prompt does not appear, check the permission status in App Break and Android Settings. Setting names can differ by phone and Android version.

Usage information and settings are handled on your device.
